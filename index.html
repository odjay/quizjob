Smart Career Orientation Quiz

1. What subjects or fields do you find most interesting?

2. Do you prefer a hands-on practical training approach or a theoretical learning approach?

3. Would you prefer a shorter training program (2 years - AFP) or a longer one (3 years - CFC)?

4. Do you prefer roles with direct customer interaction or behind-the-scenes roles?

5. Do you prefer creative work or analytical/technical tasks?

6. Do you prefer working independently or as part of a team?

7. What type of work setting do you envision?

8. How do you rate your problem-solving and communication skills?

9. Are you interested in jobs offering stability and long-term career growth?

10. Do you prefer a profession with varied daily tasks or a structured routine?

11. How important is continuous learning and professional development to you?

12. Do you see yourself working in a traditional industry or in modern digital/innovative fields?

13. Would you prefer a career with international exposure or one that is more locally focused?
